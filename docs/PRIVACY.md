# Privacy policy: VHL Text Overlay

*Last updated: November 13, 2026*

VHL Text Overlay is a Chrome extension that lets you type, highlight and draw on top of
VHL Central textbook pages, and save a picture of your work.

## What it collects

**Nothing.** The extension does not collect, store, sell or send any personal information,
browsing history, page content or anything you write or draw. It has no accounts, no
analytics, no ads and no servers. It never sends data anywhere.

## What stays on your computer, and for how long

- **Your writing and drawings** exist only on the open page. They are never saved, and they
  disappear when you reload the page or close the tab.
- **Toolbar settings** (which mode is on, the color, the text size, which side the toolbar
  is on) are kept in Chrome's temporary session storage for that tab, and are cleared when
  the tab closes.
- **Whether you've seen the tour** is a single yes/no setting stored in Chrome on your
  computer, so the tour doesn't start every time.

## Screenshots

When you click **Screenshot**, Chrome asks you to share the tab. The extension takes one
picture, stops sharing right away, and shows you the picture. It goes only where you put
it: your clipboard (**Copy**) or your Downloads folder (**Download**). Nothing is recorded
or uploaded.

## Readable colors

To choose a text color that is easy to read, the extension looks at the colors just behind
the spot where you start writing. It does this on your computer, keeps only a brightness
number, and throws the picture away immediately.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Access to `vhlcentral.com` pages | To show the toolbars and your writing on VHL Central pages only. |
| `activeTab` | After you click the extension's button, to check the colors behind your writing (see above). |
| `storage` | To remember the toolbar settings and whether you've seen the tour (see above). |
| `clipboardWrite` | To put your screenshot on the clipboard when you click **Copy**. |

## Links

The extension's welcome page and the end of its tour include an optional "Buy me a coffee"
link to buymeacoffee.com. It opens only if you click it, and the extension sends nothing
to that site.
